1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how [GAME NAME] ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses and protects information when you use our mobile game.

We respect your privacy and aim to comply with applicable privacy laws, including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act (CCPA/CPRA), and other applicable privacy regulations.

2. Information We Collect

We do not require users to create an account or register to play our game.

Depending on how you use the game and your device settings, the following information may be processed:

  • Device information, including device type, operating system and identifiers.
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it.
  • Advertising identifiers, where available and permitted.
  • Advertising interactions and information about advertisements displayed.
  • App performance, diagnostics and crash information.

Some of this information may be collected automatically by our advertising service providers.

3. Advertising – Google AdMob

Our game uses Google AdMob to display advertisements.

Google AdMob may process information such as:

  • Advertising identifiers.
  • IP addresses.
  • Device information.
  • Advertising interactions.
  • Approximate location.
  • App performance and diagnostic information.

We use personalized advertising where permitted and where the applicable consent and privacy requirements are satisfied.

Depending on applicable regulations and user privacy choices, advertising availability and ad-serving modes may vary.

Google may process information in accordance with its own privacy policy: Google Privacy Policy.

4. Consent Management – Google UMP

We use Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to manage user privacy choices and consent.

UMP helps us display relevant privacy and consent messages, including messages relating to GDPR and US state privacy regulations.

Users may be able to accept, reject or modify their privacy choices depending on the applicable message and legal requirements.

For more information: Google AdMob privacy and consent guidance.

5. Apple Game Center

Our game optionally supports Apple Game Center.

You can play the game without creating a separate account with us.

If you choose to use Game Center, Apple may process information associated with your Game Center account, such as your player identifier, nickname, achievements and leaderboard information.

Game Center is provided by Apple and is subject to Apple's Privacy Policy.

We use Game Center information only to provide the relevant game features.
